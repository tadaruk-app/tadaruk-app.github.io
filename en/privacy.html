Tadaruk – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Summary: Tadaruk does not collect any personal data about you, and does not send it to us or to anyone else. Everything you enter stays on your device only, and no one else can access it, including the provider.

1. Who we are

The Tadaruk app ("the App") is provided by Abbas Albasri ("we"). This policy explains how the App handles your data, in line with the Personal Data Protection Law of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and its Implementing Regulations.

2. Data the App handles on your device

The App stores on your device only the data needed to calculate your daily limit:

  • The name you choose for greetings.
  • Your monthly budget and the day your month starts.
  • Transactions: amount, date, merchant name when available, and your notes.
  • The last four digits of cards that appear in bank messages, and any names or notes you add to them. The App never stores full card or account numbers.
  • Plans and amounts you add yourself.
  • App settings, such as appearance, chosen companion, streak and trophies.

3. What the App does not do

  • It does not create an account or ask you to sign in, or for your phone number or email.
  • It does not send your data to any server, and we have no server that receives it.
  • It does not sell, share or rent your data to anyone.
  • It contains no ads, tracking tools, analytics, or third-party code libraries.
  • It cannot access your text messages, contacts, location or photo library.
  • It never sends you SMS messages or links, and never asks for banking details or verification codes. Any message claiming to be from the App that asks for these is not from us.

4. Bank messages and automation

The App cannot read your messages. If you create an automation yourself in Apple's Shortcuts app, your device passes the App the text of messages that contain the words you chose (such as SAR, SR or ريال). The App reads this text on your device only to extract the transaction amount:

  • If the message is a bank transaction, the App stores only the transaction data listed in section 2 on your device. It does not store the message text, only a short digital fingerprint of it to avoid recording it twice, and sends none of this to anyone.
  • If it is not a transaction (such as verification codes or promotions), the App ignores it and stores nothing from it.
  • You can stop this at any time by deleting the automation in the Shortcuts app.

5. Images and pasted text

  • When you pick an image of a bank message, the App can access only the image you picked. It reads the text in it on your device and does not store the image.
  • The App reads the clipboard only when you tap the paste button yourself.

6. Internet connection

The only connection the App makes is asking Apple's App Store for the latest version number of the App, to check whether a required update exists. This request is encrypted and contains no data about you. Apple may see general connection data such as your IP address, under Apple's own privacy policy.

7. Notifications

Notifications are local: they are created on your device and do not pass through any server. You can hide amounts from notifications and from the Lock Screen widget in the App's settings.

8. Storage and protection

  • Data is stored in a space reserved for the App and its widget only, isolated by iOS from other apps.
  • The file is encrypted with your device's encryption, and after a restart it cannot be read until the device is first unlocked with your passcode.
  • If iCloud or computer backup is on, the App's data is included in your device backup like your other apps, and is kept in your own Apple account under Apple's policy. We cannot access it.

9. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device while the App is installed. You can edit or delete any transaction or card in the App, and deleting the App removes all of its data from your device. Because your data never reaches us, we cannot recover or delete it on your behalf.

10. Your rights

The Personal Data Protection Law gives you rights, including to be informed how your data is processed, and to access, correct and destroy it. Because all of your data is on your device and under your control, you can exercise these rights directly in the App without contacting us. For any question you can reach us at the email below, and you have the right to file a complaint with the Saudi Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority (SDAIA).

11. Children

The App is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone. A minor should use it under the supervision of a parent or guardian.

12. Apple services

The App works with Apple services such as the App Store, the Shortcuts app and iCloud. These services are governed by Apple's privacy policy, which we do not control.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change how the App handles data, we will update this policy and its effective date before the change, and explain it in the App. We will not begin collecting any personal data without telling you first and obtaining your consent where required.
