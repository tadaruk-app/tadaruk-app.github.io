FAQ

Does the App read my messages?
No. You create an automation in the Shortcuts app, and it passes the App only the bank messages that contain the words you chose. The App does not store the message text.

Where is my data stored?
Only on your phone. We have no server and can never see your data.

An amount was calculated wrong. What should I do?
You can edit or delete the transaction in the App. If it keeps happening with a certain bank, email us describing the problem, without sending the real bank message or any card number.

Will the App ever ask for banking details or a verification code?
Never. Any message or call in Tadaruk's name asking for these is not from us.
